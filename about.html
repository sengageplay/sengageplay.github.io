Welcome to DoneNowPlay

Simple, visual learning games built with care for younger and neurodivergent players.

Why We Built This Space

Hello! DoneNowPlay was created out of a simple belief: every child deserves access to high-quality learning tools, without families or schools having to spend a fortune to get them.

Raising and supporting children with Special Educational Needs (SEN) comes with enough costs already. We wanted to build a calm, safe corner of the web where young minds can practice real-world skills—like matching, categorization, sentence structure, emotional awareness, and motor skills—at zero cost and with zero friction.

Our Own Symbol Library

Visual clarity is everything. To make our games as accessible as possible, we created our very own custom PECS-inspired symbol library. These clear, friendly symbols run through all our games, giving visual thinkers a consistent language they can recognize, trust, and learn from across every activity.

Our Promise to Families & Teachers

  • Always 20 Free Games: There will always be a core collection of 20 games available to play for free, anytime.
  • No Ads or Distractions: Zero pop-ups, zero flashing banners, and no hidden tracking. Just pure, calm learning.
  • Fair & Transparent Pricing: As our collection grows, we plan to offer an expanded library for a simple, one-off fee of £24.99 for lifetime access. No monthly subscriptions, ever.

Have a Game Idea? Let's Build It!

We love hearing directly from parents, teachers, therapists, and players. If there is a specific concept, daily routine, or visual skill your child or pupil is working on, tell us about it!

We regularly build custom games inspired by user ideas. As long as the idea fits our core mission—keeping things visual, accessible, low-sensory, and positive—we would love to turn your suggestion into our next game.
