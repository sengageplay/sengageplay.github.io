Why We Built This Space

DoneNowPlay was created out of a simple belief: every child deserves access to high-quality visual learning tools—without families or schools having to spend a fortune to get them.

DoneNowPlay was created out of a simple belief: every child deserves access to high-quality visual learning tools—without families or schools breaking the bank. Raising and supporting children with Special Educational Needs (SEN) comes with enough costs already. We wanted to build a calm, safe corner of the web where young minds—including those with autism, ADHD, and special educational needs (SEN)—can practice real-world skills like matching, categorization, sentence structure, emotional awareness, and motor skills. We proudly offer a core library of games completely free, with an expanded full-access collection available for a single, low, lifetime fee and zero hidden subscriptions.

Please note: As our site is in its infancy, some games may not play as smoothly on mobile devices just yet. We’re working hard behind the scenes to optimize every game for all screens, and we welcome any feedback or reported faults to help us improve!

Our Own Symbol Library

Visual clarity is everything. To make our games as accessible as possible, we created our very own custom PECS-inspired symbol library. These clear, friendly symbols run through all our games, giving visual thinkers a consistent language they can recognize, trust, and learn from across every activity.

Our Promise to Families & Teachers

  • Always 20 Free Games: There will always be a core collection of 20 games available to play for free, anytime.
  • No Ads or Distractions: Zero pop-ups, zero flashing banners, and no hidden tracking. Just pure, calm, sensory-friendly learning.
  • Fair & Transparent Pricing: As our collection grows, we plan to offer an expanded library for a simple, one-off fee of £25 for lifetime access. No monthly subscriptions, ever.
